\section*{Data availability}

The source code, the dataset commitments (Merkle trees, manifests and audit
reports), the SP1 proof provenance records, and the scripts that regenerate
every reported table are archived at
\texttt{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX} and are also available at
\url{https://github.com/patee1811/zk_offline_dqn}. The self-collected CartPole and
LunarLander replay datasets are deposited in the same record.
The public PointMaze benchmark data is not redistributed here; the archive
carries its source-integrity commitments, and the import scripts regenerate it
from Minari. Proof binaries are excluded because of their size; the provenance
records needed to regenerate and verify them are included.

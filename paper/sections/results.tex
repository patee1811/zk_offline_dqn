The evaluation is organized around three generated paper tables under
\path{artifacts/reports/final_ndss/}. The tables are produced by the experiment
scripts and checked by \path{scripts/experiments/check_report_sources.py}; the
paper does not manually rewrite benchmark numbers.

\paragraph{Table 1: RL performance.}
Table 1 reports offline-RL performance rows for the committed benchmark
datasets. Self-collected CartPole and MountainCar rows carry replay/reward
audit evidence before commitment. Public Minari/D4RL rows are
source-integrity benchmark imports only; they are not honest-collection
proofs.

\paragraph{Table 2: ZK proof cost.}
Table 2 reports proof-cost rows for supported SP1-backed relations and marks
unsupported or execute-only rows explicitly. The proof-verified rows cover TD
MVP, Merkle membership including 1k/10k/100k dataset-size scaling,
Forward-TD MLP, one-step SGD tiny, short trace, one-step training update,
training fragments $k\in\{1,4,8\}$, and proof-manifest chunk-chain
aggregation $T\in\{32,64,128\}$. Proof binaries are not committed; compact
provenance and hash inventories are.

\paragraph{Table 3: tamper rejection.}
Table 3 records tamper category, component, expected rejection layer, observed
rejection layer, and status. The mandatory categories include reward,
next-state, done flag, action, Merkle path, minibatch index, Q value, TD
target, gradient, checkpoint hash, target-network sync, and proof public
input. The current Table 3 status has no accepted-unexpectedly rows.

\paragraph{Artifact reproduction.}
The reviewer fast path is:
\begin{verbatim}
make reproduce-small
\end{verbatim}
It regenerates a tiny audited CartPole dataset commitment, validates compact
SP1 provenance and paper report sources, regenerates paper-facing summaries,
checks theorem/artifact mappings, and writes the artifact manifest. Heavy SP1
proof reruns and full benchmark reruns are opt-in through environment
variables documented in the artifact guide.

This section evaluates the system through three complementary benchmarks:
(i)~RL performance on committed datasets, (ii)~ZK proof cost for all
proof-backed relations, and (iii)~tamper rejection under adversarial
modifications.

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\textbf{Environments.} We use three environment families:
CartPole-v1 and MountainCar-v0~\cite{brockman2016gym} as self-collected
classic-control benchmarks, and PointMaze from D4RL/Minari~\cite{fu2020d4rl,
minari2023} as a public benchmark source.

\textbf{Datasets.} Self-collected datasets include CartPole-random
(9{,}204 transitions, random policy, replay/reward audited) and
MountainCar-random (10{,}000 transitions, random policy, audited). Public
benchmark datasets include PointMaze-umaze and PointMaze-umaze-dense variants
at 10k, 50k, and 100k transition scales from Minari. Public imports are
source-integrity commitments only.

\textbf{Baselines.} For RL performance, we evaluate Behavioral Cloning (BC),
Offline DQN, Double DQN, CQL-lite (discrete), BC-continuous, and IQL-lite
(continuous), each over 3 random seeds with 5{,}000 training steps.

\textbf{SP1 proving environment.} SP1 proofs were generated on Kaggle using
\texttt{cargo-prove sp1} (commit \texttt{d454975}, 2026-04-11). All benchmark
numbers are drawn from committed reports under \texttt{final\_ndss} and are not
manually rewritten.

\textbf{Regression suite.} The full Python regression runs 15 checks with 0
failures (\texttt{artifacts/regression\_summary.json}).

\textbf{Analysis.} Several patterns emerge from the proof-cost data:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Verification is fast.} All relations verify in under 0.21\,s,
  making client-side verification practical even on modest hardware.

  \item \textbf{Proof sizes are compact.} All proofs are between 2.71 and
  2.77\,MB, reflecting the STARK-based proof structure of SP1 where proof size
  grows logarithmically with computation size.

  \item \textbf{Fragment prove time scales linearly.} Prove time increases
  roughly proportionally with fragment length: $k\!=\!1$ takes 163\,s,
  $k\!=\!4$ takes 254\,s, and $k\!=\!8$ takes 441\,s, consistent with the
  per-step computation overhead.

  \item \textbf{Aggregation scales sublinearly in chunks.} The aggregation
  prove time grows from 160\,s ($T\!=\!32$) to 253\,s ($T\!=\!128$), a 1.6x
  increase for a 4x increase in steps, because the aggregation guest performs
  only hashing and consistency checks rather than re-executing training
  arithmetic.

  \item \textbf{Merkle scaling is modest.} Dataset-size scaling from canonical
  to 100k leaves increases cycles from 103k to 471k, with prove time varying
  between 122--180\,s, dominated by SP1 prover overhead rather than the Merkle
  path computation itself.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Analysis.} The tamper-rejection results demonstrate several important
properties:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{No false negatives.} Every adversarial modification across all
  17 categories is detected and rejected. There are zero ``accepted
  unexpectedly'' rows.

  \item \textbf{Multi-layer defense.} Tampering is rejected at different
  layers depending on the component and modification type: dataset audit,
  dataset commitment verification, Python semantic oracle, Rust execute mode,
  SP1 proof verification, or public-input binding checks.

  \item \textbf{Comprehensive coverage.} The tamper suite covers
  data-level attacks (reward, next state, done, action), commitment-level
  attacks (Merkle path, leaf, root), computation-level attacks (Q value, TD
  target, gradient, loss), trace-level attacks (checkpoint hash, target sync),
  and aggregation-level attacks (proof public input, manifest hash).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Artifact Reproducibility}

The reviewer fast path is:
\begin{verbatim}
  make reproduce-small
\end{verbatim}
This regenerates a tiny audited CartPole dataset commitment, validates compact
SP1 provenance and paper report sources, regenerates paper-facing summaries,
checks theorem/artifact mappings, and writes the artifact manifest. Heavy SP1
proof reruns require \texttt{RUN\_HEAVY\_SP1=1} and a Linux/WSL2/Kaggle
environment with the SP1 toolchain installed.
